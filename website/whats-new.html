Release notes

What’s new.

Windows and Android releases share a story. Full notes for every release are on GitHub.

0.22

Windows 0.22 · Android 0.8
  • A card for every look. The player card, the mini player and the phone dock each wear the look, in all 20.
  • Stencil and Film Reel. Two more motion-graphics looks: spray-paint words on concrete, and sepia film with light leaks.
  • Android widget in your look’s colours, and an Android queue so the next song can be prepared in advance where the player shares one.
  • Optional start gate (off by default): hold a song for at most a second and a half while lyrics and video are still loading.
  • An automated check that the video pauses and resumes with the music within 300 ms.

0.21

Windows 0.21 · Android 0.7
  • Sketchbook, Glitch and Pop Comic. Motion-graphics looks built on a new lyric style: one giant word at a time thrown at the camera.
  • Every look has its own screens for words without timing, no lyrics found, and a video playing behind.
  • Next-song preload on Windows with Spotify: lyrics, video and the Apple preview clip are ready before the song starts.
  • The phone’s YouTube video now pauses with the music.
  • Fixes: Terminal panes no longer overlap when a video plays; Diary is readable over dark video; covers keep thin frames in small docks.

0.20

Windows 0.20 · Android 0.6
  • The look is designed, not configured. Layout, typeface, size, spacing and animation controls are gone from Settings; each look decides.
  • A share card, a Settings panel and an animated cover frame for every look.
  • Apple preview clips cached on disk so they stop stuttering; videos that failed once are remembered for that song.
  • Synced lyrics always win over plain ones, and plain ones are re-checked for a synced version.

0.19

Windows 0.19 · Android 0.5
  • Per-look layouts: dock, floating pill, subtitle bar, chat column, terminal panes.
  • Android tablet and foldable two-pane layout, and a live home-screen widget.
  • Simpler Settings in six plain tabs, and automated screenshot tests of every look on every size.

0.18

Windows 0.18 · Android 0.4
  • Vibes arrive: Neon, Chrome, Dream, Terminal, Stickers, DM, Diary, Arcade, Cinema, Sunset Drive, Blocks, Stadium, Cassette and Trailer.
  • Cycle looks with V, or let Lyra choose by the song. A resilient lyrics and video engine with retries and a memory of your choices.
